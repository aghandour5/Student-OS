## 2024-05-24 - Context Optimization & Diamond BFS
**Learning:** Performing multiple iterations of `.includes()` on array states (`profile.progress[...].completedCourses`) within `useCallback` functions during rendering can lead to O(N^2) bottlenecks when these functions map over arrays of data. Furthermore, doing BFS on prerequisites with diamond dependencies (A->B->D and A->C->D) can lead to duplicates in the same breadth level if only checking against a global `visited` set.
**Action:** Extract list derivations safely prior to `useMemo`, memoize them into Sets (e.g. `completedCoursesSet`), and utilize `Set.has()` instead of `.includes()`. During BFS traversals that collect items per level, instantiate a local `nextLevelSet` inside the while loop to track distinct nodes enqueued during the current level iteration.
